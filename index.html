    How to turn on your microphone

    What you need: a laptop or desktop with Chrome or Edge, and this page opened from a secure address (https:// or localhost). Safari, Firefox, and most phone browsers cannot use voice answers, so type there. If you do not see "Listening" after you press Start answering, typing works the same way.

    1. Press Start answering. When your browser asks to use your microphone, choose Allow.
    2. Start talking. Your words appear in the answer box as you speak. Press Pause when you are done.

    If it says the microphone is blocked: click the lock icon on the left side of the address bar, set Microphone to Allow, then reload the page.

    For the best results: use a quiet room, speak at a normal pace, and read the text afterward to fix any words it misheard. Voice recognition is done by your browser, which in Chrome and Edge usually means the browser maker's online speech service, so your audio may leave your device. This page only keeps the text. If you review an answer, the text and your scores are kept in this browser only, under My sessions.
